About

Hi, I'm Adam.

I am in the final year of my Computer Science degree, and I am the kind of person whose interests are difficult to contain within a single field. I enjoy trying new things, building new skills, and above all understanding how things work.

I have always been drawn to science and technology. If I had not chosen Computer Science, I would probably have ended up somewhere between physics, mathematics, and engineering, ideally designing aircraft or yachts. Mathematics has been part of my life since childhood. Both of my parents are mathematicians. My father worked in theoretical mathematics and my mother in applied mathematics, so conversations about numbers, problems, and finding solutions were completely normal in our home.

I have four siblings and grew up in a very active household. I enjoy sports, travel, challenges, and situations in which I can learn through practice. Sometimes that means sailing, at other times gliding, mountains, music, or an entirely new field I previously knew little about.

Learning itself gives me a great deal of satisfaction, so I do not see my future as limited solely to work in the IT industry. I want to continue developing academically and am considering pursuing this path towards a doctorate. I am most at home in places where I can combine technical knowledge, curiosity about the world, and work on problems that do not have an immediately obvious answer.
