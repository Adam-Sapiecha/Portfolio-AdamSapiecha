About

Hi, I'm Adam.

My career focus is software engineering and backend development. I work as a Junior Automotive Software Engineer at Samsung R&D Institute and am a final-year Computer Science student at PJAIT. I build Python software, CI/CD pipelines, and tools that automate developers’ workflows.

At Samsung, I work in a team and am responsible for test automation, Jenkins, pipelines, and the test dashboard. At Sorno, I developed the backend of a system used by several thousand users across multiple schools. I am interested in the full workflow, from data and APIs to automated task execution and a clear result for the user.

My personal projects explore computer vision and geospatial processing. My posture-analysis prototype is at the body-detection and RGB-D analysis development stage. It lets me combine programming with geometry and hardware experiments. I also want to develop academically and am considering a doctorate.

Outside Computer Science, I am interested in physics, mathematics, and engineering. I have sailed since I was 13, am learning to fly gliders, and play guitar in a band. I enjoy learning through practice, whether working with a new device, sailing, or hiking in the mountains.
