Posture Analysis System

Body geometry from RGB images and stereo depth.

An OAK-D/MediaPipe pipeline combines body landmarks with depth. A separate Flask application processes uploaded RGB-D, stores history and exports PDF/JSON.

Implementation

  • Camera calibration converts pixels and depth into XYZ. Median samples and quality checks identify missing or unreliable input.
  • The camera path uses MediaPipe. The web app accepts exported landmarks or four manually marked points.
  • SQLite stores private captures, results and reports. The backend checks file ownership and data/concurrency limits.

Verification

Controlled RGB-D gives a 6 cm shoulder-height difference. Tests cover geometry, rejection, sessions, saved history and PDF export. An archived camera capture is also available below.

Images and results come from one run of an archived version and were not recalculated. Landmarks on clothing do not establish anatomical accuracy.

Limitations

A prototype without clinical validation. It requires aligned depth in mm and calibration matching RGB. The new OAK-D path has not completed human-measurement verification; MediaPipe does not directly locate C7, shoulder blades or PSIS. Guest session: 3 captures / 20 MiB, up to 24 hours.
